How it works

  1. You send the film. Hudl profile link and the game you want us to start with. Takes about three minutes.
  2. We watch it. A real evaluator, using the same Eye in the Sky framework we use for every prospect on the platform.
  3. You hear back either way. If we're moving forward, we tell you what we're building and when. If we're not, we tell you why and what would change our mind.
  4. If we scout you, coaches see it. Your report goes on the InHome platform where Directors of Player Personnel are already looking, with your score, traits, and film.

Straight answers

Does this cost anything?

No. Submitting is free and getting evaluated is free. We make our money from college programs who subscribe to the platform, not from families.

Do you really watch every one?

Yes. It's the whole point. What we can't promise is that every player gets a full report, that depends on what the tape shows.

Why won't you take my Hudl login?

Because nobody should have your password, including us. Your Hudl profile link shows us everything we need, and handing account credentials to any recruiting service is a bad habit that will get a kid burned eventually.

How long until I hear something?

We work through Friday night film over the weekend. Give us about a week in season. If we need more film to make a call, we'll ask instead of going quiet.

What if you pass on me?

You'll get a real answer about why and what would change it. A no in October isn't a no in March, plenty of players submit again after a jump and get a different answer.

I'm a JUCO or transfer player. Can I submit?

Yes. Put your current school in and note your remaining eligibility in the pitch box. We track JUCO and transfer production closely.
